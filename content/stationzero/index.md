## What StationZero is

StationZero is a home safety platform that runs at the edge.

The cameras you choose pair with a small compute node in your home. The model watches the people who live there. It knows the difference between a routine moment, something worth a quiet ping, and something that needs help right now.

You own the cameras. You own the compute. You own the recordings. You own the decisions.

## Safety, not surveillance

Surveillance is what someone else does to you with cameras. Safety is what you do for the people in your home.

StationZero is built for the second case. The footage is yours. The model that decides whether to alert you runs on your hardware. Nothing about your home leaves your network unless you explicitly send it.

## Three responses

StationZero distinguishes three kinds of moments. Each gets a different response.

**Silent.** Routine activity. Known occupants moving through the home as they always do. The system observes; you are not interrupted.

**Notify.** Something worth knowing about. A package delivered. A new face in the yard. A door opened. You get a notification — not an alarm.

**Escalate.** Something that needs help right now. A child near a hazard with no adult around. A stranger lingering after a delivery. A fall. The system escalates to emergency contact.

The line between these three is the difference between a useful safety tool and a noisy one. Most systems alarm too often, or too late. StationZero is built to do neither.

## What that looks like

Three scenarios from the launch:

- **A small child walks out by the pool. No adult is around.** StationZero escalates to emergency.
- **A package is delivered.** Notification, no alarm. **A stranger lingers in the distance afterward.** Escalation.
- **A grandparent walks through the home.** Silent. **They stumble.** Silent. **They fall.** Escalation to emergency.

The system reads context across time, not frames in isolation. The fall is escalated because of the stumble that preceded it. The lurker is escalated because of the delivery that preceded them.

## How it runs

The model lives on hardware you own. Live feeds, recording, and detection all run without a cloud account or a vendor broker. If your internet goes down, your home does not stop watching.

You decide the camera brand. You decide the storage size. You decide who has access. You decide the thresholds.
