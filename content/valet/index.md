## What Valet is

Valet is a lifelong personal intelligence that runs on your hardware. It learns you over time. Inference is unlimited and local.

You don't rent the intelligence. You own the runtime, the memory, and the boundary.

## What Valet is not

Valet is not a chatbot. Valet is not a thin client to a hosted model. Valet is not an aggregator of your accounts.

Valet runs on your machine. There is no required cloud account for core function.

## Your hardware

Valet's runtime lives on hardware you own. Continuous local inference is the design center, not a fallback. Models, embeddings, and memory state stay on disks you can read, audit, and back up.

If a remote model is invoked, that invocation is explicit, opt-in, and documented. The default is local.

## Where memory lives

Valet does not maintain its own cloud. Its long-term memory and document context route through the cloud storage you already use — Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive — or a local directory on your machine.

You already trust those tools with your files. Valet uses the same plumbing. You control retention, sharing, and revocation through the storage you already manage.
