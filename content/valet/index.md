## Most AI is a chat window you go to. Valet isn't.

Valet runs on your machine, always on. You call it — *"What's still open this week?"* — and a screen appears with the answer. You ask the next thing. The screen updates. No conversation to scroll, no panel to open, no app to switch to.

This is the difference between opening a tool and having an assistant who's there. The chat window stays closed.

## Hot mic. No cloud.

The always-listening problem isn't the always-listening. It's the always-uploading.

Valet's microphone listens because that's how voice-callable works. Nothing leaves the device. There is no transcript on someone else's computer.

This isn't a hypothetical privacy concern. Prompts to cloud AI are now subpoenable evidence — discoverable from the cloud provider, on demand. The text you typed, the responses, the history. What you typed into a chatbot to work something out can be read back later by people you weren't writing for.

Valet has nothing on anyone else's computer. There is nothing to subpoena.

When the network goes down, Valet still works. That's the test.

## It knows your particulars.

Not from a profile you fill out — from the work you actually do. Your calendar, your notes, the projects you return to, the way you ask.

When you ask what tomorrow looks like, it knows the calendars you actually use — yours, the family's, the shared ones. When you ask what's still open this week, the answer is your week — your work, your people, your particulars. When you ask what so-and-so wrote back in March, it remembers because you've been together a while.

The memory stays on your machine — yours, in your storage. Nothing about you sits on anyone else's computer. That's how the knowing keeps working when other things change.

## Yours to keep.

Buy Valet once. There is no subscription. Nothing gets gated. Your memory and your data live on your machine.

Nothing about Valet is on a recurring payment. The machine is yours. The memory is yours. Valet is yours.

## What Valet is

- **Present, not opened** — there when you call, not when you click.
- **Listens, then attends** — you speak; a screen appears with the answer.
- **Tailors each response** — the screen shows what you asked, not a transcript to scroll.
- **Keeps your particulars** — your memory, your storage, your record.
- **Stays on your hardware** — your computer, your processing, your electricity.

## What Valet is not

- **Not a chat window.** Don't go to it. It comes to you.
- **Not a remote service.** Valet runs on the machine you own.
- **Not a cloud account.** No login. No subscription tier. No data on someone else's computer.
- **Not a surveillance microphone.** The mic listens; nothing leaves.
