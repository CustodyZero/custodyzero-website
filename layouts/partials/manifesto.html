The trade

The trade isn't necessary.

The industry built useful technology on a bad trade: capability in exchange for surveillance. Your patterns. Your behavior. Your home. You became the commodity, and the product called it innovation.

Home-automation hubs send your footage upstream. Robot vacuums map your floor plan and phone it home. AI assistants store everything you ever ask. The pattern is the same — capability bought with invasion.

CustodyZero rejects the trade. Capability and custody aren't in conflict; they were paired before someone decided to separate them for someone else's revenue.
